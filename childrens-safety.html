Child Safety Standards Policy

Winmoon Technologies

Last Updated: 24 November 2026

1. Our Commitment to Child Safety

Winmoon Technologies, the developer and operator of Femmi, maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).

This policy applies to all Femmi users, profiles, messages, images, voice calls, video calls and other interactions available through the application.

2. Minimum Age Requirement

Femmi is intended exclusively for users aged 18 years or older. Individuals under 18 years of age are not permitted to register or use Femmi. Accounts identified as belonging to minors will be restricted or removed following appropriate review.

3. Prohibited Content and Conduct

Femmi strictly prohibits:

  • Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE).
  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
  • Child grooming and sexual solicitation of minors.
  • Sexual exploitation or trafficking of children.
  • Sexual extortion involving minors.
  • Creating, requesting, sharing or distributing CSAM.
  • AI-generated or digitally altered child sexual abuse material.
  • Using chat, voice calls or video calls to exploit children.

4. In-App Reporting Mechanism

Femmi provides an in-app reporting mechanism that allows users to report suspected child sexual abuse, exploitation, inappropriate content, abusive accounts and other child safety concerns directly within the app.

Users can also contact our designated Child Safety Officer by email at: good08xds@gmail.com .

Reports may include user identifiers, incident dates and descriptions of the concern. Users must not download, forward or attach suspected CSAM.

5. Investigation and Enforcement

Winmoon Technologies will prioritize child safety reports and take appropriate action, including:

  • Reviewing reported accounts and incidents.
  • Removing prohibited content.
  • Restricting or permanently banning offending accounts.
  • Preserving relevant evidence as legally required.
  • Cooperating with authorized law enforcement agencies.

6. Handling and Reporting CSAM

When Femmi obtains actual knowledge of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), we will take appropriate action to remove the material from our service, restrict responsible accounts and report confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC), where applicable, or the appropriate regional child protection or law enforcement authority.

Winmoon Technologies will cooperate with legally authorized authorities in accordance with applicable child safety laws and reporting obligations.

7. Compliance with Child Safety Laws

Femmi is committed to complying with applicable child safety laws and regulations, including applicable provisions of:

  • Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012, India.
  • Information Technology Act, 2000, India.
  • Applicable regional child protection laws.
  • Google Play Child Safety Standards Policy.

9. Privacy and Confidentiality

Child safety reports will be handled responsibly. Relevant information may be disclosed to authorized authorities when necessary to protect children or comply with applicable legal obligations.

10. Emergency Reporting

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or law enforcement immediately. Do not wait for a response from Femmi.

11. Policy Updates

Winmoon Technologies may update this policy to reflect changes in applicable laws, safety standards and application features. The latest version will remain publicly available on this website.
