# Femmi – Child Safety Standards Policy **Application Name:** Femmi **Developer / Company Name:** Winmoon Technologies **Last Updated:** 24 November 2026 ## 1. Commitment to Child Safety Winmoon Technologies is committed to maintaining a safe and responsible online environment for all users of Femmi. Femmi maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). These standards apply to all users, accounts, profiles, text messages, images, voice calls, video calls and other interactions available through Femmi. ## 2. Minimum Age Requirement Femmi is intended exclusively for individuals who are 18 years of age or older. Individuals under the age of 18 are prohibited from registering, accessing or using Femmi services. Accounts identified as belonging to minors will be restricted or removed following appropriate review. Users must not misrepresent their age or assist minors in accessing the application. ## 3. Prohibition of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) Femmi strictly prohibits any content, activity or behaviour that facilitates, promotes or involves child sexual abuse or exploitation. Prohibited activities include: - Sexual exploitation or abuse of any person under 18 years of age. - Child grooming and inappropriate sexual communication with minors. - Solicitation of sexually explicit images or videos involving minors. - Child sexual trafficking and exploitation. - Sexual extortion, coercion or threats involving minors. - Using Femmi's messaging, voice calling or video calling services to facilitate child exploitation. - Creating, distributing or requesting content that sexualizes or exploits children. Any violation of these standards may result in immediate account restrictions, permanent termination and reporting to appropriate authorities. ## 4. Prohibition of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) Femmi strictly prohibits the creation, uploading, storage, transmission, solicitation, sharing and distribution of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). This prohibition includes photographs, videos, digitally altered content, computer-generated imagery and AI-generated material depicting the sexual abuse or exploitation of children. When Femmi obtains actual knowledge of CSAM on its platform, Winmoon Technologies will take appropriate action to remove such material, restrict responsible accounts, preserve relevant evidence where legally required and report confirmed incidents to the appropriate authorities. ## 5. In-App Reporting Mechanism Femmi provides an in-app mechanism through which users can report inappropriate behaviour, abusive accounts, suspected minors, child exploitation and other child safety concerns without leaving the application. Users may also submit child safety concerns directly to our designated child safety contact: **Email:** good08xds@gmail.com Reports may include the relevant user ID, profile information, date of the incident and a description of the concern. Users must not download, reproduce, forward or attach suspected illegal child sexual abuse material. Child safety reports will be prioritized and reviewed by the responsible safety representative. ## 6. Investigation and Enforcement Winmoon Technologies will review reported child safety violations and take appropriate action based on the findings and applicable legal requirements. Enforcement measures may include: - Removal of prohibited content. - Temporary account restrictions. - Permanent account suspension or termination. - Restricting access to Femmi services. - Lawful preservation of relevant evidence. - Reporting confirmed illegal activities to appropriate authorities. - Cooperation with authorized law enforcement agencies. Femmi will take appropriate action against users who attempt to evade child safety enforcement measures. ## 7. Reporting CSAM to Relevant Authorities Winmoon Technologies maintains a process for escalating and reporting confirmed Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), where applicable, or the appropriate regional child protection or law enforcement authority. We cooperate with legally authorized agencies in investigating child sexual abuse and exploitation. If a child is in immediate danger, users should contact local emergency services or law enforcement immediately. ## 8. Compliance with Child Safety Laws Femmi is committed to complying with applicable child safety laws and regulations in the jurisdictions where its services operate. These include applicable provisions of: - The Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012, India. - The Information Technology Act, 2000, India. - Applicable child protection, online safety and reporting regulations. - Google Play's Child Safety Standards Policy. Winmoon Technologies will review and update its child safety practices as necessary to address applicable legal and regulatory requirements. ## 9.
