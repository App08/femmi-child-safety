Femmi Child Safety Standards

Policy against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) | Winmoon Technologies

1. Our commitment

Winmoon Technologies, the operator of Femmi, maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to all users and all interactions on Femmi, including profiles, messages, voice calls, and video calls.

2. Prohibited content and conduct

Femmi prohibits:

  • Creating, uploading, sharing, requesting, or distributing CSAM, including digitally generated or altered material depicting sexual exploitation of minors.
  • Grooming, coercing, manipulating, or soliciting minors for sexual activity or sexual content.
  • Sexual extortion (sextortion), trafficking, or threats involving children.
  • Using chat, voice, video, profiles, or other Femmi features to facilitate child abuse or exploitation.

3. Reporting child safety concerns

If you encounter suspected CSAE or CSAM involving Femmi, contact our designated child safety team at good08xds@gmail.com. Include the relevant user or profile identifier, date, and a description of the concern where safe and lawful. Do not forward or attach suspected CSAM.

Where Femmi offers an in-app report or support function, you may also use it to report a safety concern. If a child is in immediate danger, contact local law enforcement or emergency services without waiting for an app response.

4. Review and enforcement

Winmoon Technologies will review child safety reports, investigate as appropriate, and take action under these standards and applicable law. Actions may include restricting or suspending accounts, removing prohibited content where technically possible, preserving relevant evidence as legally required, and cooperating with competent authorities.

5. CSAM and legal compliance

When we obtain actual knowledge of CSAM on our service, we will take appropriate action under applicable law and these standards, including removal where technically possible and reporting confirmed cases to the competent authority, as required by law. We will cooperate with lawful requests from authorized agencies.

6. Safety guidance

Do not share sensitive personal information or intimate content with strangers. Report users who request sexual content involving minors, threaten others, or attempt grooming or exploitation. Do not investigate suspected abuse yourself or redistribute illegal material.
